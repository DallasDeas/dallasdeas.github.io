THE BACKSTORY

I found my direction in IT.

I went to Young Harris College expecting to build a career in finance. I earned my degree in Finance and Accounting, with a concentration in Business Analytics and a minor in Management. Over time, I became more interested in the systems behind a business: how they communicate, why they stop working, and how to keep them running securely.

That interest grew at The Tax Corner while I was in college and playing on the Young Harris baseball team. I configured Windows workstations, troubleshot connectivity and tax software, kept systems updated, and developed an encrypted backup process for sensitive client data. I liked being trusted to figure out a problem and get people back to work. It also showed me how a failed backup or a misconfigured computer could affect the business and its clients.

At Piana Technologies, I was hired as a Manufacturing Staff Accountant, but much of my work involved the company’s ERP system. I supported employees, tracked IT assets, and worked with the maintenance and business teams on parts tracking and accounts-payable workflows. I realized I wanted to work on technology full time.

My experience includes six months of Tier 1 technical support, eight months of ERP administration and accounting, and over two years supporting end users and customers in professional settings.

While working full time, I earned CCNA, CompTIA Security+, and Microsoft Azure Fundamentals. I also built labs with Cisco networking, Windows Server, Active Directory, Azure, and Entra ID. Those projects gave me a place to configure things myself, troubleshoot what broke, and document what I learned.

I’m also serving in the Army National Guard as an Officer Candidate, working toward commissioning and pursuing the Signal Corps. Communications and networking fit the direction I’m taking in my civilian career. Long term, I want to become a security engineer focused on networks, identity, and cloud infrastructure.

Right now, I’m looking for: IT support, junior networking, infrastructure support, and systems operations roles where I can contribute and keep learning.
